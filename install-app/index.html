    About this app

    Academix keeps every semester subject, its main notes PDF, previous year question papers, formula sheets, and module syllabus progress in one calm, fast study library — right on your home screen.

    Year, Semester & Branch Sorted

    Pick your academic year, semester, and branch once. Your library adapts every subject list automatically.

    Main Notes First + Helping Docs

    Open the primary subject PDF in one tap, with filterable PYQs, formula sheets, syllabi, and lab manuals underneath.

    Global Instant Search

    Search across every subject name, code, unit module, and document title straight from Home or Library.

    Protected View-Only PDF Reader

    Smooth mobile and desktop reader with zoom controls, full screen mode, and view-only protection for contributors.

    Live Module Tracker & Bookmarks

    Tick completed syllabus modules with live semester progress rings, save bookmarks, and resume your last PDF instantly.

    Contribute via Google Drive

    Share PDFs directly from your Google Drive in Profile → Contribute, track admin approval, and get credited as Contributor.
